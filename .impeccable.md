## Design Context

### Users
Jacksonville-area residents looking for a fast way to pick an outdoor pool from their phone, especially families deciding where to swim that day. Their job is to see what is open, understand the schedule quickly, and compare locations without hopping between city pages.

### Brand Personality
Public-service clear, summer-bright, and trustworthy. The interface should feel immediate and easy to read outdoors, with enough warmth to feel inviting rather than bureaucratic.

### Aesthetic Direction
Mobile-first civic utility with a bright seasonal palette, strong contrast, and an app-like bottom drawer. The map should stay central, while pool details feel tactile and structured. Avoid generic startup gradients, dark glossy glassmorphism, or tourism-brochure styling.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize map context first and details second.
2. Make current hours and season state the fastest information to scan.
3. Keep visual language warm and distinctive without sacrificing legibility in sunlight.
4. Separate source data, schedule rules, and presentation so the guide can evolve into a fuller city recreation directory.
