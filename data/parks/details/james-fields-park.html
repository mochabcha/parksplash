About James Fields Park

In 2006 design started to develop a new parking lot that will address the parking issues at the park.  A new playground was installed Summer 2023. Amenities: playground, walking path, multi purpose field, security lighting, fencing, tennis courts, basket ball court, benches, car parking, trash barrels
